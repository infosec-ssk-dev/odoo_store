What it does

One chat for the whole of your Odoo

It is not a handful of buttons bolted onto a few screens. It works out how your database is put together and takes it from there — so it handles Sales, Invoicing, Stock, HR and everything else, including the screens your own custom modules added.

🔍

Real answers, real numbers

Ask how many, how much, which ones. It goes and looks in your database every single time, so the figures are today's — never something it half-remembers.

✍

Fills things in for you

Ask it to create a quotation or update a customer and it will — after showing you exactly what it is about to change and waiting for your go-ahead.

📄

Reads what you send it

A PDF, a photo of a receipt, a scanned contract, a spreadsheet. Attach it, ask your question about it, get your answer.

📊

Writes the report

Ask for a report and you get a proper file — PDF, Excel, Word or PowerPoint — built from your real records, not retyped from memory.

🖼

Translates without wrecking the layout

Send a Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF file, ask for it in another language, and get the same document back looking exactly the same.

📈

Draws the chart

Ask for a graph and one appears in the chat — and in the PDF too, drawn from the same figures, so the two can never tell you different things.

  • “How many customer invoices are still unpaid this quarter?”
  • “Summarise this supplier contract and tell me the payment terms”
  • “Make me a 2025 sales report by salesperson, as a PDF with a chart”
  • “Translate this product sheet into Arabic and keep the layout”
  • “Which projects are running late, and who is in charge of them?”
Security

It only sees what the person asking can see

When someone asks a question, the assistant looks things up as that person. If they cannot open a record in Odoo themselves, the assistant cannot read it for them either. There is nothing to set up: it simply uses the access rights you have already given your team.

Your conversations are your own

The person who sets the assistant up looks after the AI models and the account keys. They still cannot read your chats. They can see who is using it and how often, and delete old conversations to keep things tidy — but never what was actually said. That is not just a hidden button: the database itself refuses to hand the messages over.

  • The account key stays with the administrator. Everyone else can use the AI happily without ever seeing it.
  • It cannot delete anything. Deleting records is switched off completely. The most it can do is create or change something, and only once you have said yes.
  • Passwords are hidden before anything is sent. If a field looks like a password or a key, it is blanked out before your question ever leaves Odoo.
  • Members only. Anyone outside the two AI access levels cannot get to it at all, not even with a direct link.

Or let it choose for you

Say which AI suits which kind of work, then leave the picker on Auto. A quick "hello" goes to the small cheap one; a full report goes to the strong one. And once a conversation has started on the strong one it stays there, so a short follow-up never gets you a weaker answer.

It doesn't send your whole database

For each question it first works out which part of Odoo the question is really about, and only tells the AI about that part. Faster answers, and a much smaller bill at the end of the month — even on a big database.

Files

What you can send it, and what it gives you back

You can send it

  • Documents — PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint
  • Pictures — photos and scans, in any of the usual formats
  • Text and data — plain text, CSV and the like
  • ZIP files — it opens them and reads what is inside

It gives you back

  • PDF — a plain one, or a properly designed one
  • Excel — a real spreadsheet you can keep working in
  • Word and PowerPoint
  • CSV — a straight export of whatever list you filtered

Scans and photos

Take a photo of a delivery note, or scan an invoice, and it reads it. Any AI that can look at pictures will do this — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, or one on your own server — so most of the time there is nothing extra to install. If you would rather keep it completely offline, it can use free reading software installed on your Odoo server instead, and it moves on to the next option by itself if one is not available.

Getting started

Install it, connect one AI, and you are done

Three steps

  • Install the app. Whoever installs it becomes the DIGI-ERP AI administrator straight away.
  • Open DIGI-ERP AI → AI Models and add one AI — an online one with its account key, or your own server.
  • In Settings → Users, give your colleagues the DIGI-ERP AI / User access level.

What you need

  • Odoo 18 or 19, Community or Enterprise.
  • An AI to connect to — your own, or an online one. None is included, so you stay in control of the cost.
  • Three small add-ons for Odoo that let it write Word, Excel and PDF files. Most Odoo hosting already has them; if not, your IT person installs them in a minute.
  • Two optional extras: one for PowerPoint files, one for very old .doc and .ppt files. Everything else carries on working without them.
Reference

Every setting, one by one

You will not need most of this. The app works after you connect one AI, and everything below already has a sensible value. It is here so that when you do open a screen and wonder "what does this one actually do?", the answer is written down.

Settings → DIGI-ERP AI

Reached from DIGI-ERP AI → Configuration. Only a DIGI-ERP AI administrator can open it.

Built-in local AI — optional

This whole block is for people running an AI on their own machine. If you connected an online AI under AI Models, that one takes over and you can leave everything here as it is.

Server address

Where your own AI server answers. Use http://localhost:11434 when it runs on the same machine as Odoo, or that machine's address on your network. No slash at the end.

Model

Which model on that server answers, by the name the server knows it by — for example llama3.2. It has to be downloaded there already; Odoo does not download it for you.

Answer timeout (seconds)

How long Odoo waits for an answer before giving up. Ships at 500. Raise it if long documents stop with a timeout message; a computer without a graphics card can genuinely need several minutes.

Memory per answer (tokens)

How much the model can hold in mind at once: your question, the data it looked up, and the conversation so far. Ships at 32768. Raise it if answers about long documents come back cut off — but it costs memory on your server, so raise it a step at a time.

Reading documents and images

Document reader

Which reader is tried first on a scan, a photo or a PDF. If it cannot run, the others are tried automatically, so a wrong choice here slows things down but never breaks them.

AI vision model (the default) sends the page to an AI that can look at pictures and needs nothing installed on your server. PaddleOCR, Surya and Tesseract read the page on your own machine — faster, free, and nothing leaves the building, but each one has to be installed on the Odoo server first.

AI model for reading documents

Only appears when the reader above is AI vision model. It is the AI that turns uploaded pages into text. The list offers every model you have ticked "Can read images and scans" on — online or your own, both work.

Leave it empty and the app works it out: it uses whichever AI the chat is on if that one can see pictures, and otherwise the first image-capable model you added.

Advanced

Indexing model

Used to index the shape of your Odoo — which screens and fields exist — so that each question only shows the AI the part that matters. Ships as nomic-embed-text and must be downloaded on your AI server. It reads the names of your screens and fields, never the records inside them.

Separate indexing server

Only if that indexing runs on a different machine than the chat. Leave it blank and the same server is used.

Double-check which data to look at

Off by default. Turn it on and a small, cheap AI runs first and narrows down what the main AI is shown. Every question costs one extra quick call, and answers get noticeably sharper on a database with a lot of apps installed.

Model used for the narrowing step

Only appears while the option above is on. Pick a small, fast model — this runs before every question about your data, so a heavy one cancels out the speed the step is meant to buy.

Leave it empty to use the first active model you set to "Quick questions". With nothing here and nothing set to quick questions, the step is simply skipped.

The AI model form

One card per AI, under DIGI-ERP AI → AI Models. Add as many as you like; each one shows up in the picker at the top of the chat.

The basics

Test connection

The button at the top. It calls the AI once and tells you in plain words whether the key, the address and the model name are right — so you find out here rather than in the middle of a conversation.

Name

Your own label, and the one your colleagues pick from in the chat. "Fast", "Best quality", "On-premise" are more useful to them than the manufacturer's model number.

Local model (no API key)

The switch that decides everything else on this form. On: the AI runs on your own machine or network, nothing leaves the building, no account needed. Off: you are connecting to an online service, so you pick the brand and paste a key.

Active

Switch it off to take a model out of the chat picker without deleting it — handy when a key expires or you want to stop the spending on one service for a while.

Connection

Model name

The exact name your provider uses. For example gpt-4o, claude-sonnet-4-6, gemini-2.0-flash, deepseek-chat, grok-2-latest, or on your own server whatever it is called there, such as llama3.2.

Provider

Online models only. Which company you are connecting to: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, DeepSeek, xAI (Grok), or Other / OpenAI-compatible for anything else that speaks the same language — Groq, Together, OpenRouter and so on. Picking one of the five named brands fills in the address for you.

API key

Online models only: the key from your AI account. It is stored in your own database and only an administrator can read it — a normal user can pick this model in the chat, but the key is never sent to their browser, masked or otherwise.

Base URL

Only shown when it is genuinely needed: for a model on your own server, or when the provider is Other / OpenAI-compatible. The five named brands already know their own address, so the field stays hidden and you cannot get it wrong.

What this model can do

Can read images and scans

Tick it for an AI that can look at a picture — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, qwen2.5vl and the like. When it is on, an uploaded photo or scan goes straight to that AI together with the question, and it reads the page and answers in one go.

Leave it off for text-only models. Those cannot see a picture at all, so uploaded images are handed to the document reader from the settings page first, and the AI works on the text that comes out.

Automatic model choice

Use this model for

What to send here when someone leaves the chat picker on Auto. Four kinds of work: Quick questions (greetings, short answers), Everyday work (questions about your data), Complex work (analysis, reports, creating records) and Image generation.

You do not need one model per line. A model set to complex work also handles everyday work, and an everyday model also handles quick questions — so one good model can cover the lot. Leave it empty to keep a model out of Auto entirely; it stays selectable by hand.

Advanced developer mode

This tab is hidden unless Odoo's developer mode is on, because every value in it already works. Change something here only if you know why you are changing it.

Temperature

How inventive the AI is allowed to be, from 0 (same answer every time) to 1 (creative). Ships at 0.2, which is what you want for work with real figures in it.

Max output tokens

A ceiling on how long a single answer may be. 0 means "use whatever the provider does by default", which is normally what you want.

Timeout (s)

A waiting time just for this model, when it is slower or faster than the rest. 0 means it uses the one on the settings page.

Context window

Models on your own server only: how much this one can hold in mind, when it differs from the global figure. 0 means "use the settings page". Raise it on a model you keep for long documents.

Keep alive

Models on your own server only: how long the server keeps this model loaded after a question, so the next one comes back quickly. 30m, 1h, -1 to keep it loaded for good, or 0 to free the memory at once. Blank leaves it to the server.

Supports tools

On by default. This is what lets the AI actually look things up in Odoo and prepare changes. Switch it off for a conversation-only model that cannot do that — it will still chat, but it will not be able to answer questions about your data.

Languages

It speaks your team's language

The screens come in English, French and Arabic. And whatever language you ask your question in, that is the language you get the answer in — so a French speaker and an English speaker can share the same Odoo and each be answered properly.
